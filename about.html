— ABOUT THE WRITER —

TYPED ON: A QUIET WEDNESDAY, CHENNAI. TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN — preferably someone with a script in development and twenty patient minutes.

My name is Sethu Madhavan R S. I write screenplays. I sometimes direct them. Occasionally, when the budget is tight or the role is small enough, I act in them. And every now and then a production calls and asks if I'd like to design the world the story lives in — and I almost always say yes, because building rooms is the closest most adults get to playing pretend.

I started writing the way most people start writing: badly, and with great confidence. The first script was a 124-page noir set in a town I had never visited. The second was a romantic comedy in which nobody fell in love. The third one — the third one was when I figured out that the line between intelligent and pretentious is exactly one bad metaphor wide, and I have spent every script since trying to stay on the correct side of it.

The work tends to live in three modes:

ONE. Quietly funny pieces about ordinary people behaving with unreasonable dignity.

TWO. Drama that takes its time, says less than it knows, and respects the audience enough not to underline anything.

THREE. Commercial work, unashamed and unembarrassed — because the idea that "mainstream" and "good" are opposites is one of the more recent lies the industry has been selling itself.

I write in Tamil and English, and I read in both languages plus a few I shouldn't claim. I trained at the Asian College of Journalism before I trained myself out of journalism. I have done short film festivals, writers' rooms, a brief and ill-advised season as a theatre director, and one (1) Audible audio drama as a narrator. The voiceover work paid the bills during a slow year and I am not too proud to mention it.

Things I care about: silence on the page, characters who are smarter than the plot, weather as a co-author, food as characterization, endings that earn themselves quietly, and the unfashionable belief that a film should be enjoyable.

Things I have very little patience for: villain monologues, on-the-nose voiceover, "dark for the sake of dark", and anything that would have been better as a podcast.

If you have a story you'd like me to look at, or an empty page you'd like me to fill, or a small role on a set somewhere with decent lunch — there is a contact button in the corner of every page on this site, and I will get back to you within a week.

Sometimes sooner. Rarely on Saturdays.

Yours, in good faith,
Sethu M.
